Tracing a Spark Back to Its Source

What sustains a commitment over the long haul? Jeff Kisling, a Quaker who walked the 2018 First Nation-Farmer Climate Unity March, followed his own chain of sparks back to its beginning.

Jeff tried to name the spark that got him there. Every time he found one, he realized an earlier spark had set it off. Step back through his chain.
